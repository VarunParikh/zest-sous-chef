## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Backend scaffold (FastAPI, Python 3.12), `Settings` config from `.env` with architecture §6 defaults, `.env.example`, `make setup|dev|test|scan|health`, and `GET /api/v1/health` plus a `/health` page that report text model, vision provider, fixtures and index status (block 1.1; API Spec endpoint 1, NFR-02).
- Trace module `backend.trace` writing `logs/trace.jsonl`: only the 12 data-model §11 events, strings cut to 120 characters, bytes, data URIs and base64 payloads removed (US-35; NFR-06; T-109 unit level).
- `scripts/secret_scan.py` (`make scan`): stdlib scan of the working tree and full git history for key-shaped strings, masked output, `findings: N` (T-106, S-01). Fake test values carry the `not-a-real-key` marker, which exempts only the loose `GEMINI_API_KEY=` assignment rule.
- `.gitignore` now covers `logs/`, `data/chroma/`, coverage output and generated test results; checked by T-107 (S-07).- Catalogue generator `backend.catalogue` (`mock-data/build.py`, `make data`): 1,025 variants and 2,086 SKUs from the 342-row taxonomy with seed 20261017 (committed in `mock-data/generated/`), plus a git-ignored 5,125-variant scale set. Tags are derived from the lexicon; brands are invented `MockMart` names; prices, nutrition and stock are mock values (block 1.2, US-36).
- Validators V1 to V14 (`mock-data/validate.py`): run on both sets. V9 (needs the eligibility engine, block 1.3) and V12 (no retrieval query set yet) report NOT RUN; V7 checks stock counts, and its scenario-reference part waits for `stock_scenarios.json`.
- `LeverSet` and `LEVER_OPTIONS` (`backend/session/levers.py`) with the Settings Panel option strings and ranges, used by V14.- Tranche 1 of the add-on pool (ADR-004 v2): 24 companion-course dishes D36 to D59 (9 sides, 8 beverages, 7 desserts), so 32 of 59 dishes are companions; 27 curated pairings (60 in total). Left uncovered on purpose for the vector arm: companions D27, D32, D49, D50, D51, D54, D55, D57 and non-companions D20, D23, D35 (Sushi, by design). Dishes are invented mock recipes; no culinary accuracy is claimed.
- Tranche 2 of the add-on pool: 25 companion-course dishes D60 to D84 (10 sides, 8 beverages, 7 desserts), so 57 of 84 dishes are companions; 25 more curated pairings (85 in total). New companions without a curated pair: D76, D80, D81, D82, D84. Added a fresh basil base (B352) so Caprese Salad resolves to fresh basil. Mock recipes; no culinary accuracy is claimed.
- Tranche 3: 22 companion-course dishes D85 to D106 (8 sides, 7 beverages, 7 desserts), so 79 of 106 dishes are companions; 24 more pairings (109 in total). Includes a Jain-friendly tomato chutney and a gelatin panna cotta (hidden animal ingredient, FR-91). New companions without a curated pair: D90, D91, D96, D97, D98, D105.
- Tranche 4: 21 companion-course dishes D107 to D127 (7 sides, 7 beverages, 7 desserts). The add-on pool reaches the owner target: **100 companion-course dishes, 127 dishes in total** (ADR-004 v2); 18 more pairings (127 in total). Includes egg (D112), shellfish (D113) and gluten-free (D109) companions. 26 companions and 3 other dishes (D20, D23, D35) have no curated pair, for the vector arm.
- Eligibility engine `backend.engine.eligibility` (US-28; FR-60, 90, 91, 116): diet, faith, specialised diet, declared allergen, stock and add-on price cap masks in code. Every check re-derives tags from the ingredient list and keeps the stricter value, so a mis-tagged variant cannot pass on its tags (T-019). A dish is eligible when every essential ingredient has an eligible variant; optional lines without one are dropped. The add-on price cap uses the basket cost of the dish, excluding ingredients already in the cart (C18).
- Catalogue store `backend.catalogue.store`: products, SKUs and stock, with tiered ingredient resolution (plain product first, so `milk` never resolves to kulfi; aliases such as `jowar` narrow the group term `millet`) and scripted stock overrides.
- V9 now runs on the eligibility engine: every persona has at least 3 eligible mains, 1 companion and 1 SKU.
- Scoring `backend.engine.scoring` (US-17, US-18; FR-61, 66, 111; Appendix A): eligibility first, then `S_final = Σ ŵ_k s_k` over the survivors, with rank-based `s_p`, min-max for the others (equal inputs give 0.5), `ŵ = w / Σw` (all zero restores defaults), ties by `s_p` then ID, and a breakdown whose contributions sum to the score. Context `backend.engine.context` (FR-67) builds day type, part of day, season, festive window and weather from the clock or `CONTEXT_NOW` / `CONTEXT_WEATHER`; trend rows scale by their slider group (C23). Dish features `backend.engine.features` derive history buckets, premium level and the capped gender cohort boost (C24, C26).
- `trends.csv` gains a `group` column and row T21 (summer cooling).
- Three Jain dish variants (D128 Jain Pav Bhaji, D129 Jain Dal Chawal, D130 Jain Paneer Butter Masala) with a new `variant_of` column; V9 found the Jain persona had only 2 eligible mains.

### Changed
- Variant ingredient lists carry the product form as the first qualifier when the base product name differs from its lexicon term (`milk (milk powder, skimmed)`), so different products never share an ingredient list (Data Model §1.4). Two taxonomy rows that were still duplicates got a qualifier (`=boneless` on B317 tikka, `=filter mix` on B328 chicory coffee).
- Lexicon: `sarson` is now an alias of `mustard greens` only (it was also on `mustard seeds`; V10 caught the collision); added `non-food item` for household products.
- `dishes.csv` gains `aliases`, `flavour` and `description` columns, back-filled for D01 to D35 (C20); ingredient lines accept a fourth field `opt` for non-essential garnish lines. `check_seed.py` reads the new format.

### Fixed
- Generator GI labels: pulses are low-GI (V9 found the Low-GI persona had only 2 eligible mains); spices, oils and sauces are `not_applicable` (C25). A plural base name ("Eggs", "Millets") is no longer treated as a product form.
- Nine catalogue variants whose names contradicted their derived tags (the taxonomy rule is base term plus extras) moved to correct base products B343 to B350: almond, oat and coconut milk (listed soy milk), gluten-free soy sauce (now tamari, new lexicon term), gluten-free wraps and cake mix (listed wheat or refined flour), veg seekh kebab (listed chicken), chicken sausage and bacon (listed pork). Dried kokum moved from the amchur base to its own base B351 so it resolves as kokum. A new test guards name-versus-tag agreement; two plant protein shakes are reviewed exceptions (their extras declare soy milk).
- V3 now also fails when an optional (garnish) line would break a dish's claimed diet, matching `check_seed.py`; found while building tranche 4.
- Logged C19 (`config.yaml` and `eval/thresholds.yaml` need a YAML parser that is not on the approved list) and added a block 1.1 note to C15 (vision default and health-page wording follow ADR-003 v2).
- Logged C20 (dish schema columns; resolved by the owner's choice to add columns), C21 (category and `LeverSet` vocabulary mismatches; doc-sync pending) and C22 (nine curated pairings whose `dish_b` is not a companion course; owner decision).
- C22 resolved: the owner kept the nine curated pairings.
- Logged and resolved C23 (trend groups), C24 (dish history buckets and premium level), C25 (Keto and Low-GI scope) and C26 (gender cohort boost), all by owner decision.
