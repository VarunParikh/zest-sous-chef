Fixture F25. Dish: D25 Mutton Curry.

For mutton curry for four, marinate seven hundred grams of mutton with one hundred grams of curd, twenty grams each of ginger and garlic, six grams of red chilli powder and salt. Heat forty millilitres of mustard oil, fry two hundred and fifty grams of onion until deep brown, add one hundred and fifty grams of tomato, then the mutton and eight grams of garam masala. Cook on low heat, covered, until the meat is tender, adding water as needed. Adjust the salt and serve with rice or roti.
