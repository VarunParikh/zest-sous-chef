Fixture F10. Dish: D10 Masala Dosa.

For masala dosa for three, soak two hundred grams of rice and sixty grams of urad dal separately, grind them together into a batter, and let it ferment overnight with salt. For the filling, boil three hundred grams of potato and mash it roughly. Temper mustard seeds and curry leaves in a little oil, add one hundred grams of sliced onion and two grams of turmeric, then fold in the potato with salt. Spread the batter thin on a hot griddle, drizzle forty millilitres of oil in total, add a spoon of filling, fold and serve.
