Video V1 (6YVx0i_hrZ0): Mysore Rasam. Dish: D02.

Today we are making Mysore rasam, a tangy, peppery South Indian soup. Boil about sixty grams of toor dal until it is soft and mash it well. Soak fifteen grams of tamarind in warm water and squeeze out the pulp. Dry roast a spoon of cumin and a few peppercorns, add forty grams of grated coconut and grind them into a smooth paste with a little water.

Chop one large tomato, about one hundred and fifty grams. In a pot, boil the tomato with the tamarind water, salt and a few curry leaves until the raw smell goes. Add the mashed dal, the coconut paste and enough water for three servings. Let it simmer gently, without a hard boil.

For the tempering, heat a spoon of ghee, crackle a teaspoon of mustard seeds, add a few curry leaves, and pour it over the rasam. Serve hot with rice, or drink it from a glass.
