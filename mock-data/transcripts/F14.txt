Fixture F14. Dish: D14 Aloo Paratha.

For aloo paratha for two, knead two hundred and fifty grams of wheat flour into a soft dough with water and salt. Boil and mash three hundred grams of potato, then mix it with chopped green chilli, ten grams of coriander leaves, red chilli powder and salt. Stuff a ball of dough with the filling, roll it out gently, and cook on a hot tawa with thirty grams of butter until golden on both sides. Serve with one hundred grams of curd.
