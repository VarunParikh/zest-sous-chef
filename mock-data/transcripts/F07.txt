Fixture F07. Dish: D07 Palak Paneer.

To make palak paneer for three, blanch five hundred grams of spinach for two minutes, cool it quickly and blend it to a puree. Cut two hundred and fifty grams of paneer into cubes. Heat a little oil, crackle three grams of cumin, add ten grams of chopped garlic and one hundred grams of onion, and cook until golden. Add one hundred grams of tomato, cook it down, then pour in the spinach puree and four grams of garam masala. Simmer for five minutes, add the paneer, finish with thirty millilitres of cream and salt, and serve hot.
