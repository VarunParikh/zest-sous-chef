Video V3 (xed2EfO9BRc): Keto chicken salad. Dish: D19.

This is a quick keto chicken salad with very few carbs. Season three hundred grams of chicken with salt and black pepper, and cook it in a little olive oil until golden and cooked through. Let it rest, then shred or slice it.

In a bowl, combine one hundred grams of lettuce, one hundred grams of sliced cucumber, one avocado in cubes, and forty grams of cheese. Add the chicken. For the dressing, mix twenty millilitres of olive oil with the juice of one lemon, salt and black pepper. Toss gently and serve straight away.
