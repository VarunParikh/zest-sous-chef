Video V2 (fRPDabc-Oh0): Village style chicken curry. Dish: D03.

Today's recipe is a village style chicken curry that goes well with rice, idli or dosa. Take six hundred grams of chicken, wash it, and marinate it with turmeric and salt. Heat thirty millilitres of oil, add curry leaves, then two hundred grams of chopped onion and fry until golden. Add fifteen grams each of ginger and garlic, chopped, and cook until the raw smell is gone.

Add one hundred and fifty grams of tomato, six grams of red chilli powder and eight grams of coriander powder. Cook until the oil separates. Add the chicken, mix well, cover and cook for ten minutes. Add a little water, then grind fifty grams of coconut and stir it in for the village style thickness. Simmer until the chicken is tender, check the salt, and serve hot.
