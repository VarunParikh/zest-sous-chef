Fixture F05. Dish: D05 Chicken Tikka Masala.

For chicken tikka masala for three, marinate five hundred grams of chicken in one hundred grams of curd, fifteen grams each of ginger and garlic, five grams of red chilli powder and a pinch of salt. Grill or pan sear the chicken until charred at the edges.

For the gravy, melt thirty grams of butter, fry one hundred and fifty grams of chopped onion until soft, then add three hundred grams of tomato and cook until it turns into a thick paste. Blend it smooth, return it to the pan with six grams of garam masala, and simmer. Stir in sixty millilitres of cream, add the chicken, and cook for five minutes. Adjust the salt and serve with naan.
