Fixture F22. Dish: D22 Pav Bhaji.

For pav bhaji for three, boil three hundred grams of potato, one hundred and fifty grams of cauliflower and eighty grams of green peas, then mash them together. In a pan, melt fifty grams of butter, cook one hundred grams of onion and eighty grams of capsicum, add two hundred grams of tomato and fifteen grams of pav bhaji masala, and cook until the oil separates. Add the mashed vegetables and a little water, and simmer. Toast six pav rolls in butter, squeeze lemon over the bhaji, and serve.
