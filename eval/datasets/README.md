# Evaluation datasets (drafts, pre-registration)
Status: drafted 2026-10-17, **nothing has been run**. Thresholds are in `eval/thresholds.yaml`. `MANIFEST.json` holds SHA-256 hashes; commit before the first run.

| File | Evaluation | Rows | How labels were made | Known weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `recipes_v1.jsonl` | E1 text model | 10 (5 dev, 5 test) | English lists generated from `mock-data/dishes.csv`, so labels are exact by construction; 2 Hinglish and 2 noisy transcripts hand-written by the assistant | The English lists are templated and easier than real recipes; the Hinglish set is tiny and spelling-varied |
| `utterances_v1.jsonl` | E2 intent | 60 (30/30) | Hand-written by the assistant; 4 near-miss phrases flagged | Single author; no real user data; English only |
| `guard_v1.jsonl` | E4 guard | 40 (20/20) | Hand-written: 12 injections (4 via fake transcript text), 8 off-topic, 6 hazardous, 14 benign with sensitive-sounding words | Attacks are textbook examples; a creative attacker is not represented |
| `retrieval_v1.jsonl` | E5 retrieval | 61 (31 dev, 30 test): 15 mood, 20 ingredient, 16 regional, 10 variant | Hand-written by the assistant on 2026-10-18, before any E5 run. Mood queries list every plausible dish; ingredient and regional queries expect `term:` IDs (two terms mean a clarifying question is expected); variant queries expect variant IDs from the seed-20261017 catalogue (V12 checks they exist) | Single author; relevance sets are judgement calls; MiniLM is English-centric, so the regional subset is the hard part |
| `carts_v1.jsonl` | E9 intent-to-cart | 15 | Expected ingredient families from `dishes.csv` | 10 fixture cases use own-written narrations (V1 to V4, F05 to F25); 5 text cases are generated from `dishes.csv` at run time, so those are easier than real text |
| `guard_v2.jsonl` | E4 guard, held out (C54) | 30, all test: 8 injections, 6 inside pasted or transcript text, 3 off-topic, 3 hazardous, 10 benign (4 use guardrail words harmlessly) | Written by the assistant on 2026-10-18 and hashed **before** the C54 rule was implemented; 15 of the 20 attacks avoid the rule's action words | Same author as the rule, who knew the rule when writing: biased toward what the rule catches. The owner may replace or add rows |
| `levers_v1.jsonl` | E6 lever sensitivity | 26 (20 test, 6 dev): 3 open, 7 mood, 5 stock (stock-outs set through the store's scripted overrides, C48), 5 add-on anchors (named dish) | Hand-written by the assistant on 2026-10-18, before any E6 run; each row carries the scripted intent and a pinned context (C47) | Single author; the mood vocabulary follows `dishes.csv` moods; organic acts on variants only, so it is expected to be under-exercised here (E12 measures it) |
| `persona_prompts_v1.jsonl` | E7 persona compliance | 220: 11 personas × the same 20 prompts (6 mood, 4 named dishes, 4 ingredient lists with forbidden items, 3 YouTube fixtures, 3 sample photos) | Written by the assistant on 2026-10-18 before any E7 run. `scripted_intent` and `scripted_extraction` drive the adversarial arm (transcripts always extract chicken, pork, egg, onion, garlic, potato, paneer, wine, honey, gelatin); the real-model arm ignores them | Prompts are the same for every persona; English only |
| `reasons_v1.jsonl` | E13 reason lines | 10 rows × 3 cards = 30 suggestions (5 mood prompts × P7 and P6) | Written by the assistant on 2026-10-18 before any E13 run | Small; two personas only |
| `integrity_v1.json` | E8 price and data integrity | 200 seeded carts (seed 20261018), 3 to 8 actions each | Configuration only; carts are generated at run time from the seed | Action mix is uniform, not usage-weighted |

Rules: do not tune on the test split after seeing its results; any change after the first run is logged and marked post-hoc. Labels were written by the same assistant that wrote the specification, so the owner should spot-check a sample before relying on them.
